# Billing

The [webforai platform](/platform) is metered in **credits**. Every operation reports a credit
amount; nothing else is counted — no per-seat pricing, no request tiers, no minimum commitment.

## Credit schedule

| operation | credits |
| --- | --- |
| scrape via `fetch` | 1 |
| scrape via `browser` | 2 |
| scrape via `proxy-fetch` (subscription only) | 2 |
| scrape via `proxy-browser` (subscription only) | 3 |
| `screenshot` option | +1 |
| `rehostImages` option | +1 per started 5 images |

The default [`auto` engine](/platform/api-reference#the-auto-engine) has no price of its own: the
operation bills the engine that actually produced the result (so 1 credit when a plain fetch
sufficed, 2 when the page needed browser rendering), and only that engine.

Batch and crawl jobs bill **per successfully converted page** using the same schedule. A page that
fails is not billed, and neither is a request that errors before the conversion runs.

The schedule is a single source of truth in the codebase
([`src/billing/credits.ts`](https://github.com/inaridiy/webforai/blob/main/apps/platform/src/billing/credits.ts)).

## Price

- **1,000 credits per month free** — no subscription, no card.
- Beyond that, a metered monthly subscription with graduated tiers per calendar month:

| credits in the month | price per credit | per 1,000 `fetch` pages | per 1,000 `browser` pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| first 1,000 | free | free | free |
| 1,001 – 100,000 | **$0.001** | $1.00 | $2.00 |
| 100,001 – 1,000,000 | $0.0007 | $0.70 | $1.40 |
| above 1,000,000 | $0.0005 | $0.50 | $1.00 |

Months are calendar months in UTC: the free allowance, the tiers and the spend cap all reset on the 1st at 00:00 UTC. Each tier applies only to the credits inside it. 1,000 free credits is 1,000 `fetch` scrapes, or
500 browser-rendered or `proxy-fetch` pages, every month. For example, 50,000 `auto` pages a
month where 20% need a browser is 60,000 credits: 1,000 free + 59,000 × $0.001 = **$59**.

:::note
**Price change (2026-09-24).** Prices went down: `browser` from 5 to 2 credits,
`proxy-browser` from 5 to 3, the per-credit price from $0.002 to graduated tiers starting at
$0.001, and the free allowance from 500 to 1,000 credits a month. Routing through the proxy
adds one credit to either tier.
:::

## Proxy engines need a subscription

`proxy-fetch` and `proxy-browser` — and `auto` with a non-`auto` `region` such as `"jp"`, which
runs on them — are available with an active subscription only. On the free allowance they are
refused with `402 payment_required` before anything runs, and nothing is billed. The free
allowance covers `fetch`, `browser` and `auto`. The public demo never uses the proxy.

## Spend cap

Every subscription has a monthly **spend cap**: **$50 by default**, adjustable on the
[dashboard](https://platform.webforai.dev/dashboard) from $1 to $5,000 (there is no
"unlimited"). When this calendar month's estimated bill reaches the cap, further requests
return `402 spend_cap_reached` until you raise the cap or the month ends, and a running batch
or crawl job stops instead of converting further pages. The cap is checked before each operation against
usage already recorded, so requests that are in flight at the same moment can each finish and
overshoot it by their own credits (a few cents at most in practice).

## How usage is counted

1. The API key resolves to a user, their Stripe customer and their subscription state.
2. **No active subscription** → the request is allowed only within the free monthly allowance,
   tracked against the local usage ledger for the current calendar month, and never on a proxy
   engine. Past the allowance, the API returns `402 payment_required`.
3. **Active subscription** → the request proceeds, until the month's estimated bill reaches the
   [spend cap](#spend-cap) (`402 spend_cap_reached`).
4. The operation runs. **Only on success** a usage row is written to the ledger and mirrored to a
   Stripe meter event, using the row id as the idempotency key.

The local ledger is the audit source and the dashboard's `Usage` view reads from it; the Stripe
meter is what the invoice is computed from. Unreported rows are retried automatically, so a
transient Stripe outage never loses or double-counts usage.

## Managing the subscription

Checkout and the billing portal are both reachable from the [dashboard](https://platform.webforai.dev/dashboard). Cancelling drops you back to
the free allowance rather than disabling the account.

## Self-hosting

Billing is optional. Running your own instance without `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` starts the platform in
free-allowance-only mode — everything works, nothing is metered externally, and you pay Cloudflare
and your proxy provider directly instead. If you do want metering, `pnpm stripe:setup` creates the meter and
the metered price for you. See the
[`apps/platform` README](https://github.com/inaridiy/webforai/tree/main/apps/platform#deploying-your-own-instance).
